10/01/26 Lifes Still Still Life

I have a love hate relationships with still lifes. Sometimes I think theyre boring, sometimes theyre challenging and engaging art practice, sometimes I just cant get it right, and sometimes I just cant mentally justify putting that much effort into something so boring. I think I like drawing still lifes, or ordinary things, the most when its from life. Its like wow I might be the first person ever to see these objects arranged this way in with this lighting. Its more challenging drawing from life because your head moves and sometimes your subject moves(if its a person). Also the fact the objects youre drawing just happen to be arranged, lying in this way. Theres no intentional composition, balance, or dramatic lighting. Just draw and go:) till next time.
